What this animation shows

The spiral galaxy is modeled on real astrophysics: star colors are computed from temperature via Planck's law, luminosities follow the initial mass function (IMF), and the arms are a density wave that the stars flow through. The anger level is mapped onto real galactic phenomena:

AngerIn the galaxy
Arousal levelDifferential rotation speeds up
Tension, narrowing of attentionThe arms sharpen and wind more tightly
Inner "boiling"Velocity dispersion rises and the disk thickens
Flushed faceStarburst: red H II regions glowing in H-alpha (Hα) flare up
HeartbeatThe active nucleus pulses in brightness
OutburstShock-wave ring, supernovae, red superwind
ExhaustionRotation slows and star formation dies down

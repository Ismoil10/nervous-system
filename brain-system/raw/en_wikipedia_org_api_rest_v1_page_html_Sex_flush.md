# Human sexual response cycle

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/api/rest_v1/page/html/Sex_flush

Human sexual response cycle

Four-stage model of physiological responses to sexual stimulation

The **human sexual response cycle** is a four-stage model of [physiological responses](./Physiological_response "Physiological response") to [sexual stimulation](./Sexual_stimulation "Sexual stimulation"),[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) which, in order of their occurrence, are the [excitement](./Sexual_arousal "Sexual arousal"), plateau, [orgasmic](./Orgasm "Orgasm"), and [resolution](./Refractory_period_(sex) "Refractory period (sex)") phases.[[2]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-2)[[3]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-3) This physiological response model was first formulated by [William H. Masters and Virginia E. Johnson](./Masters_and_Johnson "Masters and Johnson"), in their 1966 book *Human Sexual Response*.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1)[[4]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Masters_&_Johnson-4) Since that time, other models regarding human sexual response have been formulated by several scholars who have criticized certain inaccuracies in the human sexual response cycle model.

## Excitement phase

The **excitement phase** (also known as the **arousal phase** or **initial excitement phase**) is the first stage of the human sexual response cycle, which occurs as a result of physical or mental [erotic stimuli](./Erotic_stimuli "Erotic stimuli"), such as [kissing](./Kiss "Kiss"), [making out](./Making_out "Making out"), [fantasizing](./Sexual_fantasy "Sexual fantasy") or [viewing erotic images](./Pornography "Pornography"), that leads to [sexual arousal](./Sexual_arousal "Sexual arousal"). During this stage, the body prepares for [sexual intercourse](./Sexual_intercourse "Sexual intercourse"), initially leading to the plateau phase.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) There is wide socio-cultural variation regarding preferences for the length of foreplay and the stimulation methods used.[[5]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-5) Physical and emotional interaction and stimulation of the erogenous zones during foreplay usually establishes at least some initial arousal.[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

### Excitement in both sexes

See also: [Flushing (physiology)](./Flushing_(physiology) "Flushing (physiology)")

Among both sexes, the excitement phase results in an increase in heart rate, breathing rate, and a rise in [blood pressure](./Blood_pressure "Blood pressure").[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) A survey in 2006 found that sexual arousal in about 82% of young females and 52% of young males arises or is enhanced by direct [stimulation of nipples](./Stimulation_of_nipples "Stimulation of nipples"), with only 7–8% reporting that it decreased their arousal.[[6]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-6) [Vasocongestion](./Vasocongestion "Vasocongestion") of the skin, commonly referred to as the [sex flush](./Sex_flush "Sex flush"), will occur in approximately 50-75% of females and 25% of males. The sex flush tends to occur more often under warmer conditions and may not appear at all under cooler temperatures.

During the female sex flush, pinkish spots develop under the breasts, then spread to the breasts, torso, face, hands, soles of the feet, and possibly over the entire body.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) Vasocongestion is also responsible for the darkening of the clitoris and the walls of the vagina during sexual arousal. During the male sex flush, the coloration of the skin develops less consistently than in the female, but typically starts with the [epigastrium](./Epigastrium "Epigastrium") (upper abdomen), spreads across the chest, then continues to the neck, face, forehead, back, and sometimes, shoulders and forearms. The sex flush typically disappears soon after orgasm occurs, but this may take up to two hours or so and, sometimes, intense sweating will occur simultaneously. The flush usually diminishes in reverse of the order in which it appeared.[[4]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Masters_&_Johnson-4)

An increase in muscle tone ([myotonia](./Myotonia "Myotonia")) of certain muscle groups, occurring voluntarily and involuntarily, begins during this phase among both sexes.[[4]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Masters_&_Johnson-4)[*[clarification needed](./Wikipedia:Please_clarify "Wikipedia:Please clarify")*] Also, the [external anal sphincter](./External_anal_sphincter "External anal sphincter") may [contract](./Anal_contraction "Anal contraction") randomly upon contact (or later during [orgasm](./Orgasm "Orgasm") without contact).

### Excitement in males

In males, the beginning of the excitement phase is observed when the [penis](./Human_penis "Human penis") becomes partially or fully [erect](./Erection "Erection"), often after only a few seconds of erotic stimulation.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) The erection may be partially lost and regained repeatedly during an extended excitement phase. Both [testicles](./Testicle "Testicle") become drawn upward toward the [perineum](./Perineum "Perineum"), notably in [circumcised](./Circumcision "Circumcision") males where less skin is available to accommodate the erection. Also, the [scrotum](./Scrotum "Scrotum") can tense and thicken during the erection process.

### Excitement in females

In females, the excitement phase can last from several minutes to several hours. The onset of [vasocongestion](./Vasocongestion "Vasocongestion") results in swelling of the woman's clitoris, labia minora and vagina. The muscle that surrounds the vaginal opening grows tighter and the uterus elevates and grows in size. The vaginal walls begin to produce a [lubricating organic liquid](./Vaginal_lubrication "Vaginal lubrication").[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) Meanwhile, the breasts increase slightly in size and nipples become hardened and erect.

## Plateau phase

See also: [Edging (sexual practice)](./Edging_(sexual_practice) "Edging (sexual practice)")

The **plateau phase** is the period of sexual excitement prior to [orgasm](./Orgasm "Orgasm"). The phase is characterised by an increased circulation and heart rate in both sexes, increased sexual pleasure with increased stimulation and further increased muscle tension. Also, respiration continues at an elevated level.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) Prolonged time in the plateau phase without progression to the orgasmic phase may result in [sexual frustration](./Sexual_frustration "Sexual frustration").

### Plateau in males

During this phase, the male [urethral sphincter](./Urethral_sphincter "Urethral sphincter") contracts (preventing [urine](./Urine "Urine") from mixing with [semen](./Semen "Semen"), and [retrograde ejaculation](./Retrograde_ejaculation "Retrograde ejaculation")) and muscles at the base of the penis begin a steady rhythmic contraction.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) Males may start to secrete seminal fluid or [pre-ejaculatory](./Pre-ejaculate "Pre-ejaculate") fluid and the [testicles](./Testicles "Testicles") rise closer to the body.[[4]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Masters_&_Johnson-4)

### Plateau in females

The plateau stage in females is a continuation of the same changes evident in the excitement stage. The [clitoris](./Clitoris "Clitoris") becomes extremely sensitive and withdraws slightly, and the [Bartholin glands](./Bartholin_glands "Bartholin glands") produce further [lubrication](./Vaginal_lubrication "Vaginal lubrication"). The tissues of the outer third of the vagina swell, and the [pubococcygeus muscle](./Pubococcygeus_muscle "Pubococcygeus muscle") tightens, reducing the diameter of the opening of the vagina.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) Masters and Johnson refer to the changes that take place during the plateau stage as the [orgasmic platform](./Orgasmic_platform "Orgasmic platform").

## Orgasm phase

Main article: [Orgasm](./Orgasm "Orgasm")

Orgasm is experienced by both males and females, ending the plateau phase of the sexual response cycle. Orgasm is accompanied by quick cycles of [muscle contraction](./Muscle_contraction "Muscle contraction") in the lower [pelvic muscles](./Pelvic_muscles "Pelvic muscles"), which surround both the anus and the [primary sexual organs](./Sex_organ "Sex organ").[*[clarification needed](./Wikipedia:Please_clarify "Wikipedia:Please clarify")*] Orgasms are often associated with other *involuntary* actions, including [vocalizations](./Vocable "Vocable") and [muscular spasms](./Muscular_spasms "Muscular spasms") in other areas of the body and a generally [euphoric](./Euphoria "Euphoria") sensation. Heart rate is increased even further.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) [Tantric sex](./Tantric_sex "Tantric sex") practices may seek to diminish the goal of achieving orgasm, which is frequently a goal of sexual intercourse.

### Orgasm in males

In males, orgasm is usually associated with [ejaculation](./Ejaculation "Ejaculation"). Each ejaculation is accompanied with continuous pulses of sexual pleasure, especially in the penis and surrounding area.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) Other sensations may be felt strongly among the lower spine or lower back. The first and second convulsions are usually the most intense in sensation and produce the greatest quantity of semen. Thereafter, each contraction is associated with a diminishing volume of semen and a milder sensation of pleasure.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1)

### Orgasm in females

Women also experience [uterine](./Uterine_contraction "Uterine contraction") and [vaginal](./Vaginal_contraction "Vaginal contraction") [contractions](./Muscle_contraction "Muscle contraction"). Orgasms in females can vary widely from individual to individual. They are commonly associated with an increase in [vaginal lubrication](./Vaginal_lubrication "Vaginal lubrication"), a tightening of the vaginal walls and overall pleasure.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) For some women, there is also a possibility of [female ejaculation](./Female_ejaculation "Female ejaculation").

## Resolution phase

Main article: [Refractory period (sex)](./Refractory_period_(sex) "Refractory period (sex)")

The **resolution phase** occurs after orgasm and allows the muscles to relax, blood pressure to drop and the body to slow down from its excited state.[[1]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Archer,_Lloyd-1) The refractory period, which is part of the resolution phase, is the time frame in which usually a man is unable to orgasm again, though women can also experience a refractory period.

### Resolution in males

Masters and Johnson described the two-stage detumescence of the penis: In the first stage, the penis decreases from its erect state to about 50 percent larger than its flaccid state. This occurs during the refractory period. In the second stage (and after the refractory period is finished), the penis decreases in size and returns to being flaccid.[[4]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Masters_&_Johnson-4) It is generally impossible for men to achieve orgasm during the refractory period.[[4]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Masters_&_Johnson-4)[[7]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Rosenthal-7)[[8]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-soc-8) Masters and Johnson argue that this period must end before men can become aroused again.[[9]](./Human_sexual_response_cycle#cite_note-Dunn-9)

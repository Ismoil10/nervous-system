**Pelvic splanchnic nerves** or **nervi erigentes** are [splanchnic nerves](./Splanchnic_nerves "Splanchnic nerves") that arise from [sacral](./Spinal_nerve#Sacral_nerves "Spinal nerve") [spinal nerves](./Spinal_nerve "Spinal nerve") [S2](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_2 "Sacral spinal nerve 2"), [S3](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_3 "Sacral spinal nerve 3"), [S4](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_4 "Sacral spinal nerve 4") to provide [parasympathetic](./Parasympathetic "Parasympathetic") innervation to the organs of the [pelvic cavity](./Pelvic_cavity "Pelvic cavity").

## Structure

The pelvic splanchnic nerves arise from the [anterior rami](./Anterior_ramus_of_spinal_nerve "Anterior ramus of spinal nerve") of the sacral [spinal nerves](./Spinal_nerve "Spinal nerve") [S2](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_2 "Sacral spinal nerve 2"), [S3](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_3 "Sacral spinal nerve 3"), and [S4](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_4 "Sacral spinal nerve 4"), and enter the [sacral plexus](./Sacral_plexus "Sacral plexus").[[1]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-GRAYS2005-1)[[2]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:0-2)[[3]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:1-3) They travel to their side's corresponding [inferior hypogastric plexus](./Inferior_hypogastric_plexus "Inferior hypogastric plexus"), located bilaterally on the walls of the rectum.[[2]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:0-2)[[3]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:1-3) They contain both [preganglionic](./Preganglionic "Preganglionic") parasympathetic fibers as well as visceral afferent fibers.[[3]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:1-3) Visceral afferent fibers go to spinal cord following pathway of pelvic splanchnic nerve fibers.

The [parasympathetic nervous system](./Parasympathetic_nervous_system "Parasympathetic nervous system") is referred to as the *craniosacral outflow*; the pelvic splanchnic nerves are the *sacral* component. They are in the same region as the [sacral splanchnic nerves](./Sacral_splanchnic_nerves "Sacral splanchnic nerves"), which arise from the [sympathetic trunk](./Sympathetic_trunk "Sympathetic trunk") and provide sympathetic efferent fibers.

## Function

The pelvic splanchnic nerves contribute to the innervation of the [pelvic](./Pelvis "Pelvis") and [genital](./Genital "Genital") organs.[[2]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:0-2) The nerves regulate the emptying of the [urinary bladder](./Urinary_bladder "Urinary bladder"), control opening and closing of the [internal urethral sphincter](./Internal_urethral_sphincter "Internal urethral sphincter"), influence motility in the [rectum](./Rectum "Rectum") as well as sexual functions like [erection](./Erection "Erection").[[1]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-GRAYS2005-1)[[2]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:0-2)

In the distal 1/3 of the transverse colon, and through the sigmoid and rectum, and the [cervix](./Cervix "Cervix") in females, the pelvic splanchnic nerves supply parasympathetic function, including transmitting the sensation of pain.[[1]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-GRAYS2005-1) The proximal 2/3 of the [transverse colon](./Transverse_colon "Transverse colon"), and the rest of the proximal [gastrointestinal tract](./Gastrointestinal_tract "Gastrointestinal tract") is supplied its parasympathetic fibers by the [vagus nerve](./Vagus_nerve "Vagus nerve").

## Clinical significance

Damage to the sacral spinal nerve roots can cause [neurogenic bladder dysfunction](./Neurogenic_bladder_dysfunction "Neurogenic bladder dysfunction")[[2]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:0-2) and [fecal incontinence](./Fecal_incontinence "Fecal incontinence").[[3]](./Pelvic_splanchnic_nerves#cite_note-:1-3)

## Society and culture

The pelvic splanchnic nerves are featured as a key plot point in "[Parasites Lost](./Parasites_Lost "Parasites Lost")", a 2001 episode of the American animated TV comedy *[Futurama](./Futurama "Futurama")*; when protagonist [Philip J. Fry](./Philip_J._Fry "Philip J. Fry") is infected with [parasitic worms](./Parasitic_worm "Parasitic worm"), the rest of the crew use micro-droids to enter his body with the intention of [tickling](./Tickling "Tickling") the pelvic splanchnic [ganglion](./Ganglion "Ganglion") to trigger a convulsive [bowel movement](./Bowel_movement "Bowel movement") that they believe will expel the [worms](./Worm "Worm").

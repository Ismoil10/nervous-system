# Incertohypothalamic pathway

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/api/rest_v1/page/html/Incertohypothalamic_pathway

---

Incertohypothalamic pathway

The **incertohypothalamic pathway** is a short [dopaminergic pathway](./Dopaminergic_pathways "Dopaminergic pathways") from the [zona incerta](./Zona_incerta "Zona incerta") to the [hypothalamus](./Hypothalamus "Hypothalamus") of the brain. It has a role in modulation of fear and the integration of autonomic and neuroendocrine responses to specific sensory stimuli for example during sexual behaviour.[[1]](./Incertohypothalamic_pathway#cite_note-1)[[2]](./Incertohypothalamic_pathway#cite_note-2)[[3]](./Incertohypothalamic_pathway#cite_note-3)

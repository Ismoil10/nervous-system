The **ventral tegmental area** (**VTA**) (**tegmentum** is Latin for *covering*), also known as the **ventral tegmental area of Tsai**,[[1]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-1) or simply **ventral tegmentum**, is a group of neurons located close to the [midline](./Sagittal_plane "Sagittal plane") on the floor of the [midbrain](./Midbrain "Midbrain"). The VTA is the origin of the [dopaminergic](./Dopaminergic "Dopaminergic") cell bodies of the [mesocorticolimbic dopamine system](./Mesocorticolimbic_projection "Mesocorticolimbic projection") and other [dopamine pathways](./Dopamine_pathway "Dopamine pathway"); it is widely implicated in the drug and natural [reward circuitry](./Reward_system "Reward system") of the [brain](./Human_brain "Human brain"). The VTA plays an important role in a number of processes, including [reward cognition](./Reward_system "Reward system") ([motivational salience](./Motivational_salience "Motivational salience"), [associative learning](./Associative_learning "Associative learning"), and [positively-valenced](./Valence_(psychology) "Valence (psychology)") emotions) and [orgasm](./Orgasm "Orgasm"),[[2]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-2) among others, as well as several [psychiatric disorders](./Psychiatric_disorders "Psychiatric disorders"). Neurons in the VTA project to numerous areas of the brain, ranging from the [prefrontal cortex](./Prefrontal_cortex "Prefrontal cortex") to the caudal [brainstem](./Brainstem "Brainstem") and several regions in between.

## Structure

Neurobiologists have often had great difficulty distinguishing the VTA in humans and other primate brains from the [substantia nigra](./Substantia_nigra "Substantia nigra") (SN) and surrounding nuclei. Originally, the ventral tegmental area was designated as a ‘nucleus’, but over time ‘area’ became the more appropriate term used because of the heterogeneous [cytoarchitectonic](./Cytoarchitectonics "Cytoarchitectonics") features of the region and the lack of clear borders that separate it from adjacent regions.[[3]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-3) Because of the selective [limbic-related](./Limbic_system "Limbic system") [afferents](./Afferent_nerve_fiber "Afferent nerve fiber") to the VTA, the cells of the VTA are given the designation [A10](./Dopaminergic_cell_group_A10 "Dopaminergic cell group A10") to differentiate them from surrounding cells.[[4]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-4)

### Location

The ventral tegmental area is in the midbrain between several other major areas, some of which are described here. The [mammillary bodies](./Mammillary_bodies "Mammillary bodies") and the posterior [hypothalamus](./Hypothalamus "Hypothalamus"), both included in the [diencephalon](./Diencephalon "Diencephalon"), extend rostrally from the VTA. The [red nucleus](./Red_nucleus "Red nucleus") is situated laterally and oculomotor fibers are situated ventromedially to the VTA.[[5]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-5) The [pons](./Pons "Pons") and the [hindbrain](./Hindbrain "Hindbrain") lie caudally to the VTA. Finally, the substantia nigra is located laterally to the VTA.[[6]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-6)

### Subdivisions

In 1987, Oades identified four primary nuclei in the [VTA A10 group of cells](./Dopaminergic_cell_group_A10 "Dopaminergic cell group A10"): the nucleus paranigralis (Npn), the nucleus parabrachialis pigmentosus (Npbp), the nucleus interfascicularis (Nif), and the nucleus linearis (Nln) caudalis and rostralis. Presently, scientists divide the VTA up into four similar zones that are called the paranigral nucleus (PN), the parabrachial pigmented area (PBP), the parafasciculus retroflexus area (PFR), and the [rostromedial tegmental nucleus](./Rostromedial_tegmental_nucleus "Rostromedial tegmental nucleus") (RMTg), which approximately adhere to the previous divisions. Some definitions of the VTA also include the midline nuclei (i.e. the interfascicular nucleus, rostral linear nucleus, and central linear nucleus).

The PN and PBP are rich in dopaminergic cells, whereas the other two regions have low densities of these neurons. The PFR and RMTg contain a low density of [tyrosine hydroxylase](./Tyrosine_hydroxylase "Tyrosine hydroxylase") (TH)-positive cell bodies that are small in size and lightly stain; the RMTg is composed mostly of GABAergic cells. On the other hand, the PN and PBP consist mainly of medium to large sized TH-positive cell bodies that stain moderately.

### Inputs

Almost all areas receiving projections from the VTA project back to it. Thus, the ventral tegmental area is reciprocally connected with a wide range of structures throughout the brain suggesting that it has a role in the control of function in the [phylogenetically](./Phylogenetically "Phylogenetically") newer and highly developed [neocortex](./Neocortex "Neocortex"), as well as that of the phylogenetically older limbic areas.[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

The VTA is a heterogeneous region consisting of a variety of neurons that are characterized by different neurochemical and neurophysiological properties. Therefore, glutamatergic and GABAergic inputs are not exclusively excitatory nor inhibitory.[[7]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-ReferenceB-7) The VTA receives glutamatergic afferents from the [prefrontal cortex](./Prefrontal_cortex "Prefrontal cortex"), [pedunculopontine tegmental nucleus](./Pedunculopontine_nucleus "Pedunculopontine nucleus") (PPTg), [laterodorsal tegmental nucleus](./Laterodorsal_tegmental_nucleus "Laterodorsal tegmental nucleus"), [subthalamic nucleus](./Subthalamic_nucleus "Subthalamic nucleus"), [bed nucleus of the stria terminalis](./Bed_nucleus_of_the_stria_terminalis "Bed nucleus of the stria terminalis"), [superior colliculus](./Superior_colliculus "Superior colliculus"), [periaqueductal gray](./Periaqueductal_gray "Periaqueductal gray"), [lateral habenula](./Lateral_habenula "Lateral habenula"), [dorsal raphe nucleus](./Dorsal_raphe_nucleus "Dorsal raphe nucleus"), and [lateral hypothalamic](./Lateral_hypothalamus "Lateral hypothalamus") and [preoptic areas](./Preoptic_area "Preoptic area").[[8]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-8)[[7]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-ReferenceB-7) These glutamatergic afferents play a key role in regulating VTA cell firing. When the glutamatergic neurons are activated, the firing rates of the dopamine neurons increase in the VTA and induce burst firing. Studies have shown that these glutamatergic actions in the VTA are critical to the effects of drugs of abuse. In contrast, the [tail of the ventral tegmental area](./Tail_of_the_ventral_tegmental_area "Tail of the ventral tegmental area") (tVTA, a.k.a. the RMTg) projects to the VTA with GABAergic afferents, functioning as a "master brake" for the VTA dopamine pathways.[[9]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-VTA_tail-9)[[10]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-Brake-10)

GABAergic inputs to the VTA also include the [nucleus accumbens](./Nucleus_accumbens "Nucleus accumbens"), [ventral pallidum](./Ventral_pallidum "Ventral pallidum"), [dorsal raphe nucleus](./Dorsal_raphe_nucleus "Dorsal raphe nucleus"), [lateral hypothalamus](./Lateral_hypothalamus "Lateral hypothalamus"), [periaqueductal gray](./Periaqueductal_gray "Periaqueductal gray"), [bed nucleus of the stria terminalis](./Bed_nucleus_of_the_stria_terminalis "Bed nucleus of the stria terminalis"), and [rostromedial tegmental nucleus](./Rostromedial_tegmental_nucleus "Rostromedial tegmental nucleus") (RMTg).[[7]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-ReferenceB-7) The [lateral habenula](./Lateral_habenula "Lateral habenula") can also exert an inhibitory effect on dopaminergic neurons in the VTA by exciting RMTg GABAergic neurons, which is thought to play an important role in reward prediction errors.[[11]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-11) Subpallidal afferents into the VTA are mainly [GABAergic](./GABAergic "GABAergic") and, thus, inhibitory.[[7]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-ReferenceB-7)[[12]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-Subpallidal-12) There is a substantial pathway from the subpallidal area to the VTA.[[12]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-Subpallidal-12) When this pathway is disinhibited, an increase in the dopamine release in the [mesolimbic pathway](./Mesolimbic_pathway "Mesolimbic pathway") [amplifies locomotor activity](./Hyperlocomotion "Hyperlocomotion").[*[medical citation needed](./Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine) "Wikipedia:Identifying reliable sources (medicine)")*]

There are also cholinergic inputs to the VTA, although less studied than the glutamatergic and GABAergic inputs. Optogenetic studies in mice looking at cholinergic inputs from the [pedunculopontine tegmental nucleus](./Pedunculopontine_nucleus "Pedunculopontine nucleus") (PPTg) and the [laterodorsal tegmental nucleus](./Laterodorsal_tegmental_nucleus "Laterodorsal tegmental nucleus") demonstrate that these circuits reinforce the discharge properties of VTA neurons, suggesting a modulatory influence on reward circuits.[[13]](./Ventral_tegmental_area#cite_note-13)

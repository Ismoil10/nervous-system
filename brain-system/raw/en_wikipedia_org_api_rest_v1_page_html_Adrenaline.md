**Adrenaline**, also known as **epinephrine** and alternatively spelled **adrenalin**, is a [hormone](./Hormone "Hormone") and [medication](./Epinephrine_(medication) "Epinephrine (medication)")[[10]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-Chan_2013-10)[[11]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-11) which is involved in regulating visceral functions (e.g., respiration).[[10]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-Chan_2013-10)[[12]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-NHM_Epinephrine-12) It appears as a white microcrystalline granule.[[13]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-13) Adrenaline is normally produced by the [adrenal glands](./Adrenal_gland "Adrenal gland") and by a small number of [neurons](./Neurons "Neurons") in the [medulla oblongata](./Medulla_oblongata "Medulla oblongata").[[14]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-14) It plays an essential role in the [fight-or-flight response](./Fight-or-flight_response "Fight-or-flight response") by increasing blood flow to muscles, [heart output](./Cardiac_output "Cardiac output") by acting on the [SA node](./SA_node "SA node"),[[15]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-15) [pupil dilation response](./Pupillary_response "Pupillary response"), and [blood sugar level](./Blood_sugar_level "Blood sugar level").[[16]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-16)[[17]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-Khurana_2008-17) It does this by binding to [alpha](./Alpha_receptor "Alpha receptor") and [beta receptors](./Beta_receptors "Beta receptors").[[17]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-Khurana_2008-17) It is found in many animals, including humans, and some [single-celled organisms](./Protozoa "Protozoa").[[18]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-18)[[19]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-19) It has also been isolated from the plant *[Scoparia dulcis](./Scoparia_dulcis "Scoparia dulcis")* found in [Northern Vietnam](./Northern_Vietnam "Northern Vietnam").[[20]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-20)

## Medical uses

Main article: [Epinephrine (medication)](./Epinephrine_(medication) "Epinephrine (medication)")

As a medication, it is used to treat several conditions, including [allergic reaction](./Allergy "Allergy") [anaphylaxis](./Anaphylaxis "Anaphylaxis"), [cardiac arrest](./Cardiac_arrest "Cardiac arrest"), and superficial bleeding.[[8]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-AHFS2015-8) [Inhaled](./Nebulizer "Nebulizer") adrenaline may be used to improve the symptoms of [croup](./Croup "Croup").[[21]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-Everard_2009-21) It may also be used for [asthma](./Asthma "Asthma") when other treatments are not effective. It is given [intravenously](./Intravenously "Intravenously"), by [injection into a muscle](./Intramuscular_injection "Intramuscular injection"), by inhalation, or by [injection just under the skin](./Subcutaneous_injection "Subcutaneous injection").[[8]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-AHFS2015-8) Common side effects include shakiness, [anxiety](./Anxiety_(mood) "Anxiety (mood)"), and sweating. A fast heart rate and high blood pressure may occur. Occasionally it may result in an [abnormal heart rhythm](./Arrhythmia "Arrhythmia"). While the safety of its use during [pregnancy](./Pregnancy "Pregnancy") and [breastfeeding](./Breastfeeding "Breastfeeding") is unclear, the benefits to the mother must be taken into account.[[8]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-AHFS2015-8)

A case has been made for the use of adrenaline infusion in place of the widely accepted treatment of [inotropes](./Inotrope "Inotrope") for preterm infants with clinical [cardiovascular](./Circulatory_system "Circulatory system") compromise. Although sufficient data strongly recommends adrenaline infusions as a viable treatment, more trials are needed to conclusively determine that these infusions will successfully reduce [morbidity](./Morbidity "Morbidity") and [mortality](./Mortality_rate "Mortality rate") rates among preterm, cardiovascularly compromised infants.[[22]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-22)

Epinephrine can also be used to treat open-angle [glaucoma](./Glaucoma "Glaucoma"), as it increases the outflow of [aqueous humor](./Aqueous_humour "Aqueous humour") from the eye, which lowers [intraocular pressure](./Intraocular_pressure "Intraocular pressure").[[23]](./Adrenaline#cite_note-23)

**Dysautonomia**, **autonomic failure**, or **autonomic dysfunction** is a condition in which the [autonomic nervous system](./Autonomic_nervous_system "Autonomic nervous system") (ANS) does not work properly. This condition may affect the functioning of the [heart](./Heart "Heart"), [bladder](./Bladder "Bladder"), [intestines](./Intestines "Intestines"), [sweat glands](./Sweat_glands "Sweat glands"), [pupils](./Pupils "Pupils"), and blood vessels. Dysautonomia has many causes, not all of which may be classified as [neuropathic](./Neuropathic "Neuropathic").[[5]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-nih-5) A number of conditions can feature dysautonomia, such as [Parkinson's disease](./Parkinson's_disease "Parkinson's disease"), [Long COVID](./Long_COVID "Long COVID"), [multiple system atrophy](./Multiple_system_atrophy "Multiple system atrophy"), [dementia with Lewy bodies](./Dementia_with_Lewy_bodies "Dementia with Lewy bodies"),[[6]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-Palma2018-6) [Ehlers–Danlos syndromes](./Ehlers–Danlos_syndromes "Ehlers–Danlos syndromes"),[[7]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-7) [autoimmune autonomic ganglionopathy](./Autoimmune_autonomic_ganglionopathy "Autoimmune autonomic ganglionopathy") and [autonomic neuropathy](./Autonomic_neuropathy "Autonomic neuropathy"),[[8]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-8) [HIV/AIDS](./HIV/AIDS "HIV/AIDS"),[[9]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-9) [mitochondrial cytopathy](./Mitochondrial_cytopathy "Mitochondrial cytopathy"),[[10]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-:0-10) [pure autonomic failure](./Pure_autonomic_failure "Pure autonomic failure"), [autism](./Autism "Autism"), and [postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome](./Postural_orthostatic_tachycardia_syndrome "Postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome").[[11]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pmid38692780-11)

Diagnosis is made by functional testing of the ANS, focusing on the affected [organ system](./Organ_system "Organ system"). Investigations may be performed to identify underlying disease processes that led to the development of symptoms or autonomic neuropathy. [Symptomatic treatment](./Symptomatic_treatment "Symptomatic treatment") is available for many symptoms associated with dysautonomia, and some disease processes can be directly treated. Depending on the severity of the dysfunction, dysautonomia can range from being nearly symptomless and transient to disabling and/or life-threatening.[[12]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-aagtreatment-12)

## Signs and symptoms

Dysautonomia, a complex set of conditions characterized by autonomic nervous system (ANS) dysfunction, manifests clinically with a diverse array of symptoms of which postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS) stands out as the most common.[[11]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pmid38692780-11)

The symptoms of dysautonomia, which are numerous and vary widely by person, are due to inefficient or unbalanced [efferent](./Efferent_nerve_fiber "Efferent nerve fiber") signals sent via both systems.[*[medical citation needed](./Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine) "Wikipedia:Identifying reliable sources (medicine)")*] Symptoms in people with dysautonomia include:

- [Anhydrosis](./Anhydrosis "Anhydrosis") or [hyperhidrosis](./Hyperhidrosis "Hyperhidrosis")[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)
- Blurry or [double vision](./Diplopia "Diplopia")[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)
- [Bowel incontinence](./Bowel_incontinence "Bowel incontinence")[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)
- [Brain fog](./Clouding_of_consciousness "Clouding of consciousness")[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)
- [Constipation](./Constipation "Constipation")[[4]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pat-4)
- [Dizziness](./Dizziness "Dizziness")[[4]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pat-4)
- [Difficulty swallowing](./Dysphagia "Dysphagia")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)
- [Exercise intolerance](./Exercise_intolerance "Exercise intolerance")[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)
- [Low blood pressure](./Low_blood_pressure "Low blood pressure")[[4]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pat-4)
- [Orthostatic hypotension](./Orthostatic_hypotension "Orthostatic hypotension")[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)[[11]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pmid38692780-11)
- [Sleep apnea](./Sleep_apnea "Sleep apnea")[[4]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pat-4)
- [Syncope](./Syncope_(medicine) "Syncope (medicine)")[[4]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pat-4)
- [Tachycardia](./Tachycardia "Tachycardia")[[5]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-nih-5)
- [Tunnel vision](./Tunnel_vision "Tunnel vision")[[4]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pat-4)
- [Urinary incontinence](./Urinary_incontinence "Urinary incontinence") or [urinary retention](./Urinary_retention "Urinary retention")[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)

## Causes

Vincristine

Dysautonomia may be due to [inherited](./Heredity "Heredity") or degenerative [neurologic diseases](./Neurologic_diseases "Neurologic diseases") (primary dysautonomia)[[5]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-nih-5) or injury of the autonomic nervous system from an acquired disorder (secondary dysautonomia).[[1]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-emed-1)[[14]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-14) Its most common causes include:

- [Alcoholism](./Alcoholism "Alcoholism")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)[[15]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-15)
- [Amyloidosis](./Amyloidosis "Amyloidosis")[[4]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pat-4)[*[better source needed](./Wikipedia:Verifiability#Questionable_sources "Wikipedia:Verifiability")*]
- [Autoimmune disease](./Autoimmune_disease "Autoimmune disease"), such as [Sjögren's disease](./Sjögren's_disease "Sjögren's disease")[[16]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-16)[[17]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-17)[[18]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-18)[[19]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-19) or [systemic lupus erythematosus](./Systemic_lupus_erythematosus "Systemic lupus erythematosus") (lupus), and [autoimmune autonomic ganglionopathy](./Autoimmune_autonomic_ganglionopathy "Autoimmune autonomic ganglionopathy")[*[citation needed](./Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]
- [Craniocervical instability](./Craniocervical_instability "Craniocervical instability")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)
- [Diabetes](./Diabetes "Diabetes")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)
- [Eaton–Lambert syndrome](./Eaton–Lambert_syndrome "Eaton–Lambert syndrome")[*[medical citation needed](./Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine) "Wikipedia:Identifying reliable sources (medicine)")*]
- [Ehlers–Danlos syndrome](./Ehlers–Danlos_syndrome "Ehlers–Danlos syndrome")[[20]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-20)
- [Guillain–Barré syndrome](./Guillain–Barré_syndrome "Guillain–Barré syndrome")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)[[21]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-21)[[22]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-22)
- [HIV/AIDS](./HIV/AIDS "HIV/AIDS")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)
- [Long COVID](./Long_COVID "Long COVID")[[23]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-Paliwal2020-23)[[24]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-pmid36412084-24)
- [Multiple sclerosis](./Multiple_sclerosis "Multiple sclerosis"), [meningitis-retention syndrome](./Meningitis-retention_syndrome "Meningitis-retention syndrome")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)
- [Paraneoplastic syndrome](./Paraneoplastic_syndrome "Paraneoplastic syndrome")[[25]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-Mayo_Clinic_—_Paraneoplastic_Syndrome-25)
- [Spinal cord injury](./Spinal_cord_injury "Spinal cord injury")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13) or [traumatic brain injury](./Traumatic_brain_injury "Traumatic brain injury")[[26]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-concussionalliance.org-26)
- [Synucleinopathy](./Synucleinopathy "Synucleinopathy"), a group of neurodegenerative diseases including [pure autonomic failure](./Pure_autonomic_failure "Pure autonomic failure"), [Parkinson's disease](./Parkinson's_disease "Parkinson's disease"), [dementia with Lewy bodies](./Dementia_with_Lewy_bodies "Dementia with Lewy bodies") and [multiple system atrophy](./Multiple_system_atrophy "Multiple system atrophy")[[6]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-Palma2018-6)
- [Surgery](./Surgery "Surgery") or [injury](./Injury "Injury") involving the [nerves](./Nerves "Nerves")[[13]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-med-13)
- [Toxicity](./Toxicity "Toxicity") ([vincristine](./Vincristine "Vincristine"))[[27]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-27)

In the [sympathetic nervous system](./Sympathetic_nervous_system "Sympathetic nervous system") (SNS), predominant dysautonomia is common along with [fibromyalgia](./Fibromyalgia "Fibromyalgia"), [chronic fatigue syndrome](./Chronic_fatigue_syndrome "Chronic fatigue syndrome"), [irritable bowel syndrome](./Irritable_bowel_syndrome "Irritable bowel syndrome"), and [interstitial cystitis](./Interstitial_cystitis "Interstitial cystitis"), raising the possibility that such dysautonomia could be their common clustering underlying [pathogenesis](./Pathogenesis "Pathogenesis").[[28]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-28)

In addition to sometimes being a symptom of dysautonomia, [anxiety](./Anxiety#cite_ref-WHO2009_6-2 "Anxiety") can sometimes physically manifest symptoms resembling autonomic dysfunction.[[29]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-29)[[30]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-30)[[31]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-31) A thorough investigation ruling out physiological causes is crucial, but in cases where relevant tests are performed and no causes are found or symptoms do not match any known disorders, a primary anxiety disorder is possible but should not be presumed.[[32]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-32) For such patients, the [anxiety sensitivity](./Anxiety_sensitivity "Anxiety sensitivity") index may have better predictivity for anxiety disorders while the [Beck Anxiety Inventory](./Beck_Anxiety_Inventory "Beck Anxiety Inventory") may misleadingly suggest anxiety for patients with dysautonomia.[[33]](./Dysautonomia#cite_note-33)

The **spinothalamic tract** is a [nerve tract](./Nerve_tract "Nerve tract") in the [anterolateral system](./Anterolateral_system "Anterolateral system") in the [spinal cord](./Spinal_cord "Spinal cord").[[1]](./Spinothalamic_tract#cite_note-Haines-1) This tract is an ascending sensory pathway to the [thalamus](./Thalamus "Thalamus"). From the [ventral posterolateral nucleus](./Ventral_posterolateral_nucleus "Ventral posterolateral nucleus") in the thalamus, sensory information is relayed upward to the [somatosensory cortex](./Somatosensory_cortex "Somatosensory cortex") of the [postcentral gyrus](./Postcentral_gyrus "Postcentral gyrus").

The spinothalamic tract consists of two adjacent pathways: anterior and lateral. The anterior spinothalamic tract carries information about [crude touch](./Crude_touch "Crude touch"). The lateral spinothalamic tract conveys [pain](./Pain "Pain") and [temperature](./Temperature "Temperature").[[2]](./Spinothalamic_tract#cite_note-Al-Chalabi-2)

In the [spinal cord](./Spinal_cord "Spinal cord"), the spinothalamic tract has [somatotopic organization](./Somatotopic_arrangement "Somatotopic arrangement"). This is the segmental organization of its [cervical](./Neck "Neck"), [thoracic](./Thoracic "Thoracic"), [lumbar](./Lumbar "Lumbar"), and [sacral](./Sacrum "Sacrum") components, which is arranged from most medial to most lateral respectively.

The pathway crosses over ([decussates](./Decussates "Decussates")) at the level of the spinal cord, rather than in the [brainstem](./Brainstem "Brainstem") like the [dorsal column-medial lemniscus pathway](./Dorsal_column-medial_lemniscus_pathway "Dorsal column-medial lemniscus pathway") and [lateral corticospinal tract](./Lateral_corticospinal_tract "Lateral corticospinal tract"). It is one of the three tracts which make up the [anterolateral system](./Spinothalamic_tract#Anterolateral_system): anterior and lateral spinothalamic tract, [spinotectal tract](./Spinotectal_tract "Spinotectal tract"), [spinoreticular tract](./Spinoreticular_tract "Spinoreticular tract").

## Structure

The anterior and lateral spinothalamic tracts labelled at lower right as tracts of the anterolateral system.

There are two main parts of the spinothalamic tract:

- The [lateral spinothalamic tract](./Lateral_spinothalamic_tract "Lateral spinothalamic tract") transmits [pain](./Pain "Pain") and [temperature](./Temperature "Temperature").
- The [anterior spinothalamic tract](./Anterior_spinothalamic_tract "Anterior spinothalamic tract") (or *ventral* spinothalamic tract) transmits crude touch and firm pressure.

The spinothalamic tract, like the [dorsal column-medial lemniscus pathway](./Dorsal_column-medial_lemniscus_pathway "Dorsal column-medial lemniscus pathway"), uses three neurons to convey sensory information from the periphery to conscious level at the cerebral cortex.

[Pseudounipolar](./Pseudounipolar "Pseudounipolar") [neurons](./Neuron "Neuron") in the [dorsal root ganglion](./Dorsal_root_ganglion "Dorsal root ganglion") have axons that lead from the [skin](./Skin "Skin") into the dorsal [spinal cord](./Spinal_cord "Spinal cord") where they ascend or descend one or two vertebral levels via [Lissauer's tract](./Lissauer's_tract "Lissauer's tract") and then [synapse](./Synapse "Synapse") with secondary neurons in either the [substantia gelatinosa of Rolando](./Substantia_gelatinosa_of_Rolando "Substantia gelatinosa of Rolando") or the [nucleus proprius](./Nucleus_proprius "Nucleus proprius"). These secondary neurons are called *tract cells*.

The axons of the tract cells cross over (decussate) to the other side of the spinal cord via the [anterior white commissure](./Anterior_white_commissure "Anterior white commissure"), and to the anterolateral corner of the spinal cord (hence the spinothalamic tract being part of the [anterolateral system](./Anterolateral_system "Anterolateral system")). Decussation usually occurs 1-2 spinal nerve segments above the point of entry. The axons travel up the length of the spinal cord into the [brainstem](./Brainstem "Brainstem"), specifically the [rostral ventromedial medulla](./Rostral_ventromedial_medulla "Rostral ventromedial medulla").

Traveling up the brainstem, the tract moves dorsally. The neurons ultimately synapse with third-order neurons in several nuclei of the thalamus—including the medial dorsal, ventral posterior lateral, and ventral posterior medial nuclei. From there, signals go to the [cingulate cortex](./Cingulate_cortex "Cingulate cortex"), the [primary somatosensory cortex](./Primary_somatosensory_cortex "Primary somatosensory cortex"), and [insular cortex](./Insular_cortex "Insular cortex") respectively.

### Anterior spinothalamic tract

The **anterior spinothalamic tract** (Latin: *tractus spinothalamicus anterior*) or **ventral spinothalamic fasciculus** situated in the marginal part of the [anterior funiculus](./Anterior_funiculus "Anterior funiculus") and intermingled more or less with the [vestibulospinal tract](./Vestibulospinal_tract "Vestibulospinal tract"), is derived from cells in the [posterior column](./Posterior_column "Posterior column") or intermediate [gray matter](./Gray_matter "Gray matter") of the opposite side. [Aβ](./Type_II_sensory_fiber "Type II sensory fiber") fibres carry sensory information pertaining to crude touch from the skin. After entering the spinal cord the first order neurons synapse (in the nucleus proprius), and the second order neurons decussate via the [anterior white commissure](./Anterior_white_commissure "Anterior white commissure"). These second order neurons ascend synapsing in the VPL of the thalamus. Incoming first order neurons can ascend or descend via the Lissauer tract.

Its fibers convey crude touch information to the VPL ([ventral posterolateral nucleus](./Ventral_posterolateral_nucleus "Ventral posterolateral nucleus")) of the [thalamus](./Thalamus "Thalamus").

The fibers of the anterior spinothalamic tract conduct information about pressure and crude touch (protopathic). The fine touch (epicritic) is conducted by fibers of the medial lemniscus. The medial lemniscus is formed by the axons of the neurons of the gracilis and cuneatus nuclei of the medulla oblongata which receive information about light touch, vibration and conscient proprioception from the gracilis and cuneatus fasciculus of the spinal cord. This fasciculus receive the axons of the first order neuron which is located in the dorsal root ganglion that receives [afferent fibers](./Afferent_nerve_fiber "Afferent nerve fiber") from receptors in the skin, muscles and joints.

High-resolution RNA sequencing finds the anterior spinothalamic tract has five distinct types of neurons. Three clusters of which are located mainly in laminae I–III of the dorsal horn and two clusters in deeper laminae.[[3]](./Spinothalamic_tract#cite_note-k847-3)

### Lateral spinothalamic tract

The **lateral spinothalamic tract** (or **lateral spinothalamic fasciculus**), is a bundle of [afferent nerve fibers](./Afferent_nerve_fiber "Afferent nerve fiber") ascending through the white matter of the [spinal cord](./Spinal_cord "Spinal cord"), in the spinothalamic tract, carrying sensory information to the [brain](./Human_brain "Human brain"). It carries pain, and temperature sensory information ([protopathic sensation](./Protopathic_sensation "Protopathic sensation")) to the [thalamus](./Thalamus "Thalamus"). It is composed primarily of fast-conducting, sparsely [myelinated](./Myelinated "Myelinated") [A delta fibers](./A_delta_fiber "A delta fiber") and slow-conducting, unmyelinated [C fibers](./Group_C_nerve_fiber "Group C nerve fiber"). These are secondary sensory neurons which have already [synapsed](./Synapse "Synapse") with the primary [sensory neurons](./Sensory_neuron "Sensory neuron") of the [peripheral nervous system](./Peripheral_nervous_system "Peripheral nervous system") in the [posterior horn of the spinal cord](./Posterior_horn_of_spinal_cord "Posterior horn of spinal cord") (one of the three [grey columns](./Grey_column "Grey column")).

There is evidence to suggest the existence of a projection from lamina I and deeper layers of the dorsal horn to the ventrobasal complex and other thalamic nuclei, eventually passing pain and temperature information to the SI and SII somatosensory cortices.[[4]](./Spinothalamic_tract#cite_note-4) In macaque monkeys, neurons from the posterior part of the ventral medial nucleus terminate at the posterior half of the superior limiting sulcus that bounds the dorsal insular cortext.[[5]](./Spinothalamic_tract#cite_note-5)

Together with the anterior spinothalamic tract, the lateral spinothalamic tract is sometimes termed the *secondary sensory fasciculus* or *spinal lemniscus*.

**Interstitial cells of Cajal** (ICC) are [interstitial cells](./Interstitial_cells "Interstitial cells") embedded in the *muscularis propria* (muscular layer) of the [gastrointestinal tract](./Gastrointestinal_tract "Gastrointestinal tract"). There are different types of ICC with different functions. ICC and another type of interstitial cell, known as [platelet-derived growth factor receptor alpha](./Platelet-derived_growth_factor_receptor_alpha "Platelet-derived growth factor receptor alpha") (PDGFRα) cells, are electrically coupled to [smooth muscle cells](./Smooth_muscle_cells "Smooth muscle cells") via [gap junctions](./Gap_junction "Gap junction"), that work together as an [SIP functional syncytium](./Syncytium#Smooth_muscle "Syncytium").[[2]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-Song-2) [Myenteric](./Myenteric "Myenteric") interstitial cells of Cajal (ICC-MY) serve as pacemaker cells that generate the bioelectrical events known as [slow waves](./Slow-wave_potential "Slow-wave potential").[[3]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-3) Slow waves conduct to smooth muscle cells and cause phasic contractions.[[4]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-pmid20059699-4)[[5]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-Sanders-5)

An isolated interstitial cell of Cajal from the [myenteric plexus](./Myenteric_plexus "Myenteric plexus") of the mouse small intestine grown in a primary cell culture.

The picture to the right shows an isolated interstitial cell of Cajal from the myenteric plexus of the mouse small intestine grown in a primary cell culture. This cell type can be characterized morphologically as having a small cell body often triangular or stellate-shaped with several long processes branching out into secondary and tertiary extensions - these processes often contact smooth muscle cells. They have contractile behaviour in both the cell body and the extended processes.

## Embryology

These cells are derived from [mesoderm](./Mesoderm "Mesoderm"),[[6]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-Turksen2006-6) unlike the [enteric neurons](./Enteric_neuron "Enteric neuron") that arise from neural crest cells (ie, [ectoderm](./Ectoderm "Ectoderm")).

## Function

Intramuscular interstitial cells of Cajal (ICC-IM) are involved in mediating responses to neurotransmission.[[7]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-pmid21757854-7) All ICC in the gastrointestinal tract express calcium-activated chloride channels encoded by the gene *ANO1*. These channels are activated by release of calcium in ICC and are important for both the pacemaker activity of ICC and their responses to neurotransmitters. A recent review noted that [carbachol](./Carbachol "Carbachol") increases ICC activity through this channel.[[8]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-ICC_review-8)[[9]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-pmid21768263-9) ANO1-knockout mice fail to produce slow waves and ANO1 channel inhibitors block slow waves.[[8]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-ICC_review-8)

ICC are also thought to be present in other types of smooth muscle tissues. But with few exceptions the function of these cells is not well understood and currently an area of active [research](./Research "Research").

### Role in slow wave activity

See also: [Basal electrical rhythm](./Basal_electrical_rhythm "Basal electrical rhythm")

ICC serve as [electrical](./Electricity "Electricity") pacemakers and generate spontaneous electrical [slow waves](./Slow_wave_threshold "Slow wave threshold") in the gastrointestinal (GI) tract.[[10]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-Kaki2023-10) Electrical slow waves spread from ICC to [smooth muscle cells](./Smooth_muscle_cell "Smooth muscle cell") and the resulting depolarization initiates [calcium ion](./Calcium_ion "Calcium ion") entry and contraction. Slow waves organize gut contractions into phasic contractions that are the basis for [peristalsis](./Peristalsis "Peristalsis") and [segmentation](./Segmentation_contractions "Segmentation contractions").[*[medical citation needed](./Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine) "Wikipedia:Identifying reliable sources (medicine)")*]

### Frequency of ICC pacemaker cells

The frequency of ICC pacemaker activity differs in different regions of the GI tract:[[11]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-11)

- 3 per minute in the [stomach](./Stomach "Stomach")
- 12 per minute in the [duodenum](./Duodenum "Duodenum")
- 9 per minute in the [ileum](./Ileum "Ileum")
- 3 per minute in the [colon](./Colon_(anatomy) "Colon (anatomy)")

ICC also mediate neural input from [enteric](./Bowels "Bowels") [motor neurons](./Motor_neuron "Motor neuron"). Animals lacking ICC have greatly reduced responses to the [neurotransmitter](./Neurotransmitter "Neurotransmitter") [acetylcholine](./Acetylcholine "Acetylcholine"), released from excitatory motor neurons, and to the [transmitter](./Cell_signaling "Cell signaling") [nitric oxide](./Nitric_oxide "Nitric oxide"), released from [inhibitory motor neurons](./Inhibitory_motor_neuron?action=edit&redlink=1 "Inhibitory motor neuron"). Loss of ICC in [disease](./Disease "Disease"), therefore, may interrupt normal neural control of gastrointestinal (GI) contractions and lead to [functional GI disorders](./Functional_GI_disorder?action=edit&redlink=1 "Functional GI disorder"), such as [irritable bowel syndrome](./Irritable_bowel_syndrome "Irritable bowel syndrome").[*[medical citation needed](./Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine) "Wikipedia:Identifying reliable sources (medicine)")*]

ICC also express mechano-sensitive mechanisms that cause these cells to respond to stretch. Stretching GI muscles can affect the [resting potentials](./Resting_potential "Resting potential") of ICC and affect the frequency of pacemaker activity. [Carbachol](./Carbachol "Carbachol") increases ICC activity through [ANO1](./ANO1 "ANO1") activation.[[8]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-ICC_review-8)[[9]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-pmid21768263-9)

ICC are also critical in the propagation of electrical slow waves. ICC form a network through which slow wave activity can propagate. If this network is broken, then 2 regions of muscle will function independently.[*[medical citation needed](./Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine) "Wikipedia:Identifying reliable sources (medicine)")*]

## Pathology

ICCs are thought to be the cells from which [gastrointestinal stromal tumours](./Gastrointestinal_stromal_tumour "Gastrointestinal stromal tumour") (GISTs) arise.[[12]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-12) Also, abnormalities in the ICC network is one cause of [chronic intestinal pseudo-obstruction](./Chronic_intestinal_pseudo-obstruction "Chronic intestinal pseudo-obstruction").[[13]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-degiorgio-13)

## Eponym

The interstitial cells of Cajal are named after [Santiago Ramón y Cajal](./Santiago_Ramón_y_Cajal "Santiago Ramón y Cajal"),[[14]](./Interstitial_cell_of_Cajal#cite_note-14) a [Spanish](./Spanish_people "Spanish people") [pathologist](./Pathologist "Pathologist") and [Nobel laureate](./Nobel_laureate "Nobel laureate").

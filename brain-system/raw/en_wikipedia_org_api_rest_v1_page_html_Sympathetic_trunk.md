The **sympathetic trunk** (**sympathetic chain**, **gangliated cord**) is a paired bundle of nerve fibers that run from the base of the [skull](./Skull "Skull") to the [coccyx](./Coccyx "Coccyx"). It is a major component of the [sympathetic nervous system](./Sympathetic_nervous_system "Sympathetic nervous system").

## Structure

The sympathetic trunk lies just lateral to the [vertebral bodies](./Vertebral_bodies "Vertebral bodies") for the entire length of the [vertebral column](./Vertebral_column "Vertebral column"). It interacts with the [anterior rami](./Anterior_ramus_of_spinal_nerve "Anterior ramus of spinal nerve") of [spinal nerves](./Spinal_nerves "Spinal nerves") by way of [rami communicantes](./Rami_communicantes "Rami communicantes"). The sympathetic trunk permits [preganglionic fibers](./Preganglionic_fibers "Preganglionic fibers") of the [sympathetic nervous system](./Sympathetic_nervous_system "Sympathetic nervous system") to ascend to spinal levels superior to T1 and descend to spinal levels inferior to L2/3.[[1]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-1)[[2]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-2)[[3]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-3)[[4]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-4)[[5]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-5)[[6]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-6)[[7]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-7)

The superior end of it is continued upward through the [carotid canal](./Carotid_canal "Carotid canal") into the skull, and forms a [plexus](./Plexus "Plexus") on the [internal carotid artery](./Internal_carotid_artery "Internal carotid artery"); the inferior part travels in front of the [coccyx](./Coccyx "Coccyx"), where it converges with the other trunk at a structure known as the [ganglion impar](./Ganglion_impar "Ganglion impar").

Along the length of the sympathetic trunk are [sympathetic ganglia](./Sympathetic_ganglia "Sympathetic ganglia") known as [paravertebral ganglia](./Paravertebral_ganglia "Paravertebral ganglia").

- The sympathetic trunk (yellow) can be seen just [lateral](./Anatomical_terms_of_location#lateral "Anatomical terms of location") to the [vertebral column](./Human_vertebral_column "Human vertebral column").

### Relations

In the cervical region, the sympathetic trunk is situated upon the [prevertebral fascia](./Prevertebral_fascia "Prevertebral fascia") posterior to the [carotid sheath](./Carotid_sheath "Carotid sheath").[[8]](./Sympathetic_trunk#cite_note-:3-8): 600

## Function

The sympathetic trunk is a fundamental part of the [sympathetic nervous system](./Sympathetic_nervous_system "Sympathetic nervous system"), and part of the [autonomic nervous system](./Autonomic_nervous_system "Autonomic nervous system"). It allows nerve fibres to travel to spinal nerves that are superior and inferior to the one in which they originated. Also, a number of nerves, such as most of the [splanchnic nerves](./Splanchnic_nerves "Splanchnic nerves"), arise directly from the trunks.

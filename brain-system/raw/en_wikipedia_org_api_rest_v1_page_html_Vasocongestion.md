**Vasocongestion**, **vascular congestion** or **vascular engorgement** is the swelling of bodily tissues caused by increased [vascular](./Blood_vessel "Blood vessel") [blood](./Blood "Blood") flow and a localized increase in [blood pressure](./Blood_pressure "Blood pressure"). Typical causes of vasocongestion in humans includes [menstruation](./Menstruation "Menstruation"), [sexual arousal](./Sexual_arousal "Sexual arousal"), [REM sleep](./REM_sleep "REM sleep"), strong [emotions](./Emotion "Emotion"), [illnesses](./Illness "Illness"), [deep venous thrombosis](./Deep_venous_thrombosis "Deep venous thrombosis") (DVT) and [allergic reactions](./Allergy "Allergy").

## Illnesses

[Menstrual cramps](./Menstrual_cramp "Menstrual cramp") are one of the most common side effects of vasocongestion in adult females. Although pain and discomfort varies among individuals, vasocongestion is an essential part of the shedding of the lining of the [uterus](./Uterus "Uterus"). Abnormal vasocongestion during the menstrual cycle can lead to irregular bleeding, severe and debilitating cramps and [anemia](./Anemia "Anemia") caused by increased menstrual flow.

The temporary vasocongestion of the [cheeks](./Cheek "Cheek") of the [face](./Face "Face") is called [blushing](./Blushing "Blushing"). It may be caused by the emotions of [anger](./Anger "Anger") or [embarrassment](./Embarrassment "Embarrassment"). The illness of [rosacea](./Rosacea "Rosacea") is a chronic vasocongestion condition of the face that primarily affects the cheeks and nose, but may include other areas such as the eyes and chin.

Vasocongestion of the area around the [human anus](./Human_anus "Human anus") can lead to the formation of [hemorrhoids](./Hemorrhoids "Hemorrhoids").[*[medical citation needed](./Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine) "Wikipedia:Identifying reliable sources (medicine)")*]

## Human sexual behavior

Vasocongestion is essential for [sexual procreation in mammals](./Mammalian_reproduction "Mammalian reproduction") since this is the process that enables the hardening of a [penis](./Penis "Penis") during an [erection](./Erection "Erection"). The same force leads to the [hardening of the clitoris](./Clitoral_erection "Clitoral erection") and [vaginal lubrication](./Vaginal_lubrication "Vaginal lubrication") during sexual arousal.[[1]](./Vasocongestion#cite_note-1) The decrease in vasocongestion in post-[menopausal](./Menopause "Menopause") women may require some women to use artificial [sexual lubricant](./Sexual_lubricant "Sexual lubricant") to avoid pain during [sexual intercourse](./Sexual_intercourse "Sexual intercourse"). If there is pain however, sexual drive is typically diminished or absent.

Other forms of vasocongestion during human sexual activity include [sex flush](./Sex_flush "Sex flush") and the swelling of [nipples](./Nipple "Nipple") in both men and women.[[2]](./Vasocongestion#cite_note-2)

Unpleasant side effects of sex-related vasocongestion accompanied by sexual inactivity may lead to the [cramp](./Cramp "Cramp")-like pain of "[blue balls](./Blue_balls "Blue balls")"[[3]](./Vasocongestion#cite_note-3) in males and an unpleasant [pelvic](./Pelvis "Pelvis") "heaviness" in women,[[4]](./Vasocongestion#cite_note-4) similar to the start of the menstruation cycle.

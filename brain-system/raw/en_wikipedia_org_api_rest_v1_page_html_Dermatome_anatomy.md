A **dermatome** is an area of [skin](./Skin "Skin") that is mainly supplied by [afferent nerve fibres](./Afferent_nerve_fibres "Afferent nerve fibres") from the [dorsal root](./Dorsal_root_of_spinal_nerve "Dorsal root of spinal nerve") of any given [spinal nerve](./Spinal_nerve "Spinal nerve").[[1]](./Dermatome_(anatomy)#cite_note-1)[[2]](./Dermatome_(anatomy)#cite_note-2)
There are 8 [cervical nerves](./Cervical_nerves "Cervical nerves") (C1 being an exception with no dermatome),
12 [thoracic nerves](./Thoracic_nerves "Thoracic nerves"),
5 [lumbar nerves](./Lumbar_nerves "Lumbar nerves") and 5 [sacral nerves](./Sacral_nerves "Sacral nerves").
Each of these nerves relays sensation (including pain) from a particular region of skin to the [brain](./Brain "Brain").

The term is also used to refer to a part of an embryonic [somite](./Somite "Somite").

Along the [thorax](./Thorax "Thorax") and [abdomen](./Abdomen "Abdomen"), the dermatomes are like a stack of discs forming a human, each supplied by a different spinal nerve. Along the arms and the legs, the pattern is different: the dermatomes run longitudinally along the limbs. Although the general pattern is similar in all people, the precise areas of innervation are as unique to an individual as fingerprints.

An area of skin innervated by a single [nerve](./Peripheral_nervous_system "Peripheral nervous system") is called a [peripheral nerve field](./Peripheral_nerve_field "Peripheral nerve field").

The word *dermatome* is formed from [Ancient Greek](./Ancient_Greek "Ancient Greek") δέρμα 'skin, hide' and τέμνω 'cut'.

## Clinical significance

[Referred pain](./Referred_pain "Referred pain"): Conscious perception of visceral sensations is referred to specific regions of the body that are not sources of the sensations. Some referred pain due to visceral sensations refer to dermatomes that send fibers to the same level of the spinal cord.

A dermatome is an area of skin supplied by sensory neurons that arise from a spinal nerve ganglion. Symptoms that follow a dermatome (e.g., pain or a rash) may indicate a pathology that involves the related [nerve root](./Nerve_root "Nerve root"). Examples include somatic dysfunction of the spine or viral infection. Certain skin problems tend to orient lesions along dermatomal lines.

In [referred pain](./Referred_pain "Referred pain"), [sensory nerve fibers](./Sensory_nerve_fibers "Sensory nerve fibers") such as those from dermatomes may come together at the same [spinal cord](./Spinal_cord "Spinal cord") level as the [general visceral afferent fibers](./General_visceral_afferent_fibers "General visceral afferent fibers") such as those from the [heart](./Heart "Heart").
When the general visceral sensory fiber is stimulated, the [central nervous system](./Central_nervous_system "Central nervous system") does not clearly discern whether the [pain](./Pain "Pain") is coming from the body wall or from the [viscera](./Viscera "Viscera"), so it perceives the pain as coming from somewhere on the body wall, e.g., left arm/hand pain, jaw pain.
So the pain is "referred to" the related dermatomes of the same spinal segment.[[3]](./Dermatome_(anatomy)#cite_note-3)

Viruses that lie dormant in nerve ganglia (e.g., [varicella zoster virus](./Varicella_zoster_virus "Varicella zoster virus"), which causes both [chickenpox](./Chickenpox "Chickenpox") and [shingles](./Shingles "Shingles")) often cause either pain, rash, or both in a pattern defined by a dermatome (a zosteriform pattern). However, the symptoms may not appear across the entire dermatome.

## Important dermatomes and anatomical landmarks

Following is a list of [spinal nerves](./Spinal_nerve "Spinal nerve") and points that are characteristically belonging to the dermatome of each nerve:[[4]](./Dermatome_(anatomy)#cite_note-4)

Dermatomes of the lower limb (modified, from Fender, after Foerster)

- [C2](./Cervical_spinal_nerve_2 "Cervical spinal nerve 2") – At least one cm lateral to the [occipital protuberance](./External_occipital_protuberance "External occipital protuberance") at the [base of the skull](./Base_of_the_skull "Base of the skull"). Alternately, a point at least 3 cm (1.2 in) behind the ear.
- [C3](./Cervical_spinal_nerve_3 "Cervical spinal nerve 3") – In the [supraclavicular fossa](./Supraclavicular_fossa "Supraclavicular fossa"), at the [midclavicular line](./Midclavicular_line "Midclavicular line").
- [C4](./Cervical_spinal_nerve_4 "Cervical spinal nerve 4") – Over the [acromioclavicular joint](./Acromioclavicular_joint "Acromioclavicular joint").
- [C5](./Cervical_spinal_nerve_5 "Cervical spinal nerve 5") – On the lateral (radial) side of the [antecubital fossa](./Antecubital_fossa "Antecubital fossa"), just proximally to the elbow.
- [C6](./Cervical_spinal_nerve_6 "Cervical spinal nerve 6") – On the dorsal surface of the [proximal phalanx](./Proximal_phalanges "Proximal phalanges") of the [thumb](./Thumb "Thumb").
- [C7](./Cervical_spinal_nerve_7 "Cervical spinal nerve 7") – On the dorsal surface of the [proximal phalanx](./Proximal_phalanges "Proximal phalanges") of the [middle finger](./Middle_finger "Middle finger").
- [C8](./Cervical_spinal_nerve_8 "Cervical spinal nerve 8") – On the dorsal surface of the proximal phalanx of the [little finger](./Little_finger "Little finger").
- [T1](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_1 "Thoracic spinal nerve 1") – On the medial (ulnar) side of the [antecubital fossa](./Antecubital_fossa "Antecubital fossa"), just distal to the [medial epicondyle of the humerus](./Medial_epicondyle_of_the_humerus "Medial epicondyle of the humerus").
- [T2](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_2 "Thoracic spinal nerve 2") – At the apex of the [axilla](./Axilla "Axilla").
- [T3](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_3 "Thoracic spinal nerve 3") – [Intersection](./Line-line_intersection "Line-line intersection") of the [midclavicular line](./Midclavicular_line "Midclavicular line") and the third [intercostal space](./Intercostal_space "Intercostal space")
- [T4](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_4 "Thoracic spinal nerve 4") – Intersection of the [midclavicular line](./Midclavicular_line "Midclavicular line") and the fourth intercostal space, located at the level of the nipples.
- [T5](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_5 "Thoracic spinal nerve 5") – Intersection of the midclavicular line and the fifth intercostal space, horizontally located midway between the level of the nipples and the level of the [xiphoid process](./Xiphoid_process "Xiphoid process").
- [T6](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_6 "Thoracic spinal nerve 6") – Intersection of the midclavicular line and the horizontal level of the [xiphoid process](./Xiphoid_process "Xiphoid process").
- [T7](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_7 "Thoracic spinal nerve 7") – Intersection of the midclavicular line and the horizontal level at one quarter the distance between the level of the xiphoid process and the level of the [umbilicus](./Navel "Navel").
- [T8](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_8 "Thoracic spinal nerve 8") – Intersection of the midclavicular line and the horizontal level at one half the distance between the level of the xiphoid process and the level of the umbilicus.
- [T9](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_9 "Thoracic spinal nerve 9") – Intersection of the midclavicular line and the horizontal level at three quarters of the distance between the level of the xiphoid process and the level of the umbilicus.
- [T10](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_10 "Thoracic spinal nerve 10") – Intersection of the midclavicular line, at the horizontal level of the umbilicus.
- [T11](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_11 "Thoracic spinal nerve 11") – Intersection of the midclavicular line, at the horizontal level midway between the level of the umbilicus and the [inguinal ligament](./Inguinal_ligament "Inguinal ligament").
- [T12](./Thoracic_spinal_nerve_12 "Thoracic spinal nerve 12") – Intersection of the midclavicular line and the midpoint of the inguinal ligament.
- [L1](./Lumbar_spinal_nerve_1 "Lumbar spinal nerve 1") – Midway between the key sensory points for T12 and L2.[*[clarification needed](./Wikipedia:Please_clarify "Wikipedia:Please clarify")*]
- [L2](./Lumbar_spinal_nerve_2 "Lumbar spinal nerve 2") – On the anterior medial thigh, at the midpoint of a line connecting the midpoint of the inguinal ligament and the [medial epicondyle of the femur](./Medial_epicondyle_of_the_femur "Medial epicondyle of the femur").
- [L3](./Lumbar_spinal_nerve_3 "Lumbar spinal nerve 3") – At the [medial epicondyle of the femur](./Medial_epicondyle_of_the_femur "Medial epicondyle of the femur").
- [L4](./Lumbar_spinal_nerve_4 "Lumbar spinal nerve 4") – Over the [medial malleolus](./Medial_malleolus "Medial malleolus").
- [L5](./Lumbar_spinal_nerve_5 "Lumbar spinal nerve 5") – On the [dorsum of the foot](./Foot_dorsum "Foot dorsum") at the third [metatarsophalangeal joint](./Metatarsophalangeal_joint "Metatarsophalangeal joint").
- [S1](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_1 "Sacral spinal nerve 1") – On the lateral aspect of the [calcaneus](./Calcaneus "Calcaneus").
- [S2](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_2 "Sacral spinal nerve 2") – At the midpoint of the [popliteal fossa](./Popliteal_fossa "Popliteal fossa").
- [S3](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_3 "Sacral spinal nerve 3") – Over the [tuberosity of the ischium](./Tuberosity_of_the_ischium "Tuberosity of the ischium") or [intragluteal fold](./Intragluteal_fold?action=edit&redlink=1 "Intragluteal fold")
- [S4](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_4 "Sacral spinal nerve 4") and [S5](./Sacral_spinal_nerve_5 "Sacral spinal nerve 5") – In the [perianal](./Perianal "Perianal") area, less than one cm lateral to the [mucocutaneous zone](./Mucocutaneous_zone "Mucocutaneous zone")

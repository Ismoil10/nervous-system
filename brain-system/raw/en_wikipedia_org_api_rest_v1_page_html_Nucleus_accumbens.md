The **nucleus accumbens** (**NAc** or **NAcc**; also known as the **accumbens nucleus**, or formerly as the ***nucleus accumbens septi***, [Latin](./Latin "Latin") for '[nucleus](./Nucleus_(neuroanatomy) "Nucleus (neuroanatomy)") adjacent to the [septum](./Septum_pellucidum "Septum pellucidum")') is a region in the [basal forebrain](./Basal_forebrain "Basal forebrain") [rostral](./Rostral_(anatomical_term) "Rostral (anatomical term)") to the [preoptic area](./Preoptic_area "Preoptic area") of the [hypothalamus](./Hypothalamus "Hypothalamus").[[1]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Carlson,_Neil_R_2013-1) The nucleus accumbens and the [olfactory tubercle](./Olfactory_tubercle "Olfactory tubercle") collectively form the [ventral striatum](./Ventral_striatum "Ventral striatum"). The ventral striatum and [dorsal striatum](./Dorsal_striatum "Dorsal striatum") collectively form the [striatum](./Striatum "Striatum"), which is the main component of the [basal ganglia](./Basal_ganglia "Basal ganglia").[[2]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-2) The [dopaminergic neurons](./Dopaminergic_pathways "Dopaminergic pathways") of the [mesolimbic pathway](./Mesolimbic_pathway "Mesolimbic pathway") project onto the [GABAergic](./GABAergic "GABAergic") [medium spiny neurons](./Medium_spiny_neuron "Medium spiny neuron") of the nucleus accumbens and olfactory tubercle.[[3]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Dopaminergic_pathways_and_reward_system_review-3)[[4]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-NAcc_function-4) Each [cerebral hemisphere](./Cerebral_hemisphere "Cerebral hemisphere") has its own nucleus accumbens, which can be divided into two structures: the nucleus accumbens core and the nucleus accumbens shell. These substructures have different morphology and functions.

Different NAcc subregions (core vs shell) and neuron subpopulations within each region ([D1-type](./D1-type "D1-type") vs [D2-type](./D2-type "D2-type") medium spiny neurons) are responsible for different [cognitive functions](./Cognition "Cognition").[[5]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-NAcc_core_and_shell-5)[[6]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Reward_and_aversion_MSNs-6) As a whole, the nucleus accumbens has a significant role in the cognitive processing of [motivation](./Motivational_salience "Motivational salience"), [aversion](./Aversives "Aversives"), [reward](./Reward_system "Reward system") (i.e., [incentive salience](./Incentive_salience "Incentive salience"), [pleasure](./Pleasure "Pleasure"), and [positive reinforcement](./Positive_reinforcement "Positive reinforcement")), and [reinforcement](./Reinforcement "Reinforcement") learning (e.g., [Pavlovian-instrumental transfer](./Pavlovian-instrumental_transfer "Pavlovian-instrumental transfer"));[[4]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-NAcc_function-4)[[7]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Aversion_neurons-7)[[8]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Malenka_NAcc-8)[[9]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Mesolimbic_dopamine_in_motivation_and_PIT-9)[[10]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Learning+Motivation_in_PIT_-_neural_mechanisms_review-10) hence, it has a significant role in [addiction](./Addiction "Addiction").[[4]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-NAcc_function-4)[[8]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Malenka_NAcc-8) In addition, part of the nucleus accumbens core is centrally involved in the induction of [slow-wave sleep](./Slow-wave_sleep "Slow-wave sleep").[[11]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Zinc_&_sleep_2017_review-11)[[12]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Nucleus_accumbens_-_Slow-wave_sleep-12)[[13]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Slow-wave_sleep_and_NAcc_core_A2AR_neurons_-_Sept_2017_primary_source-13)[[14]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Striatal_A2AR_neurons_and_sleep_-_Oct_2017_primary_source-14) The nucleus accumbens plays a lesser role in processing [fear](./Fear "Fear") (a form of aversion), [impulsivity](./Impulsivity "Impulsivity"), and the [placebo effect](./Placebo_effect "Placebo effect").[[15]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-15)[[16]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-placebo_effect-16)[[17]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-pmid20831892-17) It is involved in the encoding of new [motor programs](./Motor_function "Motor function") as well.[[4]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-NAcc_function-4)

## Structure

The nucleus accumbens is an aggregate of neurons which is described as having an outer shell and an inner core.[[4]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-NAcc_function-4)

### Input

Major [glutamatergic](./Glutamatergic "Glutamatergic") inputs to the nucleus accumbens include the [prefrontal cortex](./Prefrontal_cortex "Prefrontal cortex") (particularly the [prelimbic cortex](./Prelimbic_cortex "Prelimbic cortex") and [infralimbic cortex](./Infralimbic_cortex "Infralimbic cortex")), [basolateral amygdala](./Basolateral_amygdala "Basolateral amygdala"), ventral [hippocampus](./Hippocampus "Hippocampus"), [thalamic](./Thalamic "Thalamic") nuclei (specifically the [midline thalamic nuclei](./Midline_thalamic_nuclei "Midline thalamic nuclei") and [intralaminar nuclei of the thalamus](./Intralaminar_nuclei_of_the_thalamus "Intralaminar nuclei of the thalamus")), and glutamatergic projections from the [ventral tegmental area](./Ventral_tegmental_area "Ventral tegmental area") (VTA).[[18]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Rapid+transient_drug-induced_synaptic_plasticity-18) The nucleus accumbens receives [dopaminergic inputs](./Dopamine_pathway "Dopamine pathway") from the ventral tegmental area, which connect via the [mesolimbic pathway](./Mesolimbic_pathway "Mesolimbic pathway"). The nucleus accumbens is often described as one part of a [cortico-basal ganglia-thalamo-cortical loop](./Cortico-basal_ganglia-thalamo-cortical_loop "Cortico-basal ganglia-thalamo-cortical loop").[[19]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Striatal_efferents,_afferents,_and_colocalized_receptors_in_dMSNs_and_iMSNs-19)

Dopaminergic inputs from the VTA modulate the activity of [GABAergic](./GABAergic "GABAergic") neurons within the nucleus accumbens. These neurons are activated directly or indirectly by [euphoriant](./Euphoriant "Euphoriant") drugs (e.g., [amphetamine](./Amphetamine "Amphetamine"), [opiates](./Opiate "Opiate"), etc.) and by participating in rewarding experiences (e.g., sex, music, exercise, etc.).[[20]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Nestler-20)[[21]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-ΔFosB_reward-21)

Another major source of input comes from the CA1 and ventral [subiculum](./Subiculum "Subiculum") of the [hippocampus](./Hippocampus "Hippocampus") to the [dorsomedial](./Dorsomedial_area "Dorsomedial area") area of the nucleus accumbens. Slight depolarizations of cells in the nucleus accumbens correlates with positivity of the neurons of the hippocampus, making them more excitable. The correlated cells of these excited states of the medium spiny neurons in the nucleus accumbens are shared equally between the subiculum and CA1. The subiculum neurons are found to hyperpolarize (increase negativity) while the CA1 neurons "ripple" (fire > 50 Hz) in order to accomplish this priming.[[22]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-22)

The nucleus accumbens is one of the few regions that receives a high density of histaminergic projections from the [tuberomammillary nucleus](./Tuberomammillary_nucleus "Tuberomammillary nucleus") (the sole source of [histamine](./Histamine "Histamine") neurons in the brain).[[23]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Histamine_pathways-23)

### Output

The output neurons of the nucleus accumbens send [axonal projections](./Axonal_projection "Axonal projection") to the [basal ganglia](./Basal_ganglia "Basal ganglia") and the ventral analog of the [globus pallidus](./Globus_pallidus "Globus pallidus"), known as the [ventral pallidum](./Ventral_pallidum "Ventral pallidum") (VP). The VP, in turn, projects to the [medial dorsal nucleus](./Medial_dorsal_nucleus "Medial dorsal nucleus") of the dorsal [thalamus](./Thalamus "Thalamus"), which projects to the prefrontal cortex as well as back to the ventral and to dorsal [striatum](./Striatum "Striatum"). Other efferents from the nucleus accumbens include connections with the [tail of the ventral tegmental area](./Tail_of_the_ventral_tegmental_area "Tail of the ventral tegmental area"),[[24]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Brake-24) [substantia nigra](./Substantia_nigra "Substantia nigra"), and the [reticular formation](./Reticular_formation "Reticular formation") of the [pons](./Pons "Pons").[[1]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Carlson,_Neil_R_2013-1)

### Shell

The **nucleus accumbens shell** (**NAcc shell**) is a substructure of the nucleus accumbens. The shell and core together form the entire nucleus accumbens.

**Location:** The shell is the outer region of the nucleus accumbens, and – unlike the core – is considered to be part of the [extended amygdala](./Extended_amygdala "Extended amygdala"), located at its rostral pole.

**Cell types:** Neurons in the nucleus accumbens are mostly [medium spiny neurons](./Medium_spiny_neuron "Medium spiny neuron") (MSNs) containing mainly [D1-type](./D1-type "D1-type") (i.e., [DRD1](./DRD1 "DRD1") and [DRD5](./DRD5 "DRD5")) or [D2-type](./D2-type "D2-type") (i.e., [DRD2](./DRD2 "DRD2"), [DRD3](./DRD3 "DRD3"), and [DRD4](./DRD4 "DRD4")) [dopamine receptors](./Dopamine_receptor "Dopamine receptor"). A subpopulation of MSNs contain both D1-type and D2-type receptors, with approximately 40% of striatal MSNs expressing both [DRD1](./DRD1 "DRD1") and [DRD2](./DRD2 "DRD2") mRNA.[[19]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Striatal_efferents,_afferents,_and_colocalized_receptors_in_dMSNs_and_iMSNs-19)[[25]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Ventral_striatum-25)[[26]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-MSN_40%_mixed-type_with_DRD1_and_DRD2-26) These mixed-type NAcc MSNs with both D1-type and D2-type receptors are mostly confined to the NAcc shell.[[19]](./Nucleus_accumbens#cite_note-Striatal_efferents,_afferents,_and_colocalized_receptors_in_dMSNs_and_iMSNs-19) The neurons in the shell, as compared to the core, have a lower density of [dendritic spines](./Dendritic_spine "Dendritic spine"), less terminal segments, and less branch segments than those in the core.
